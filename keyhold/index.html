Keyhold

Everything that matters, in one file that backs itself up.

Keyhold is a password manager and authenticator for Windows. Passwords, two-factor codes and important files such as SSH keys and recovery codes live together in one encrypted file on your computer.

Passwords and two-factor codes

Keeps logins and shows the current two-factor code next to each one. Types them into any window with Ctrl+Alt+K.

Browser extension

Fills logins and codes right under the field in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and saves new logins as you sign in. It only talks to Keyhold on the same computer.

Important files

Watches folders like .ssh and copies every change into the vault, so a lost disk does not mean lost keys.

Backups

Every change is copied to the folders you choose. Optionally, the encrypted file is also kept in your own Google Drive so your other devices stay in sync.

Two-factor codes from a QR code

Reads the QR code from the screen or an image, including the export from Google Authenticator.

Private by design

The vault is encrypted with AES-256 on your device. Nothing is sent to the author — there is no Keyhold server, no account and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
