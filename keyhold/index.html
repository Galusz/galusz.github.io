Keyhold

Everything that matters, in one file that backs itself up.

Keyhold is a password manager and authenticator for Windows and Android. Passwords, two-factor codes and important files such as SSH keys and recovery codes live together in one encrypted file, kept in step between your devices through your own Google Drive.

Passwords and two-factor codes

Keeps logins and two-factor codes, like Google Authenticator. Pin a code to a login or a site and it is offered right where it is needed. Types logins and codes into any window with Ctrl+Alt+K.

Browser extension

Fills logins and codes right under the field in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and offers to save a new login once it worked. It works with the Keyhold app on the same computer — or on its own, opening your vault from your Google Drive with the master password.

On the phone

Your codes and logins on Android, filled into apps and browsers from the suggestion under the field. New logins are offered for saving and reach your computer through Drive.

Important files

Watches folders like .ssh and copies every change into the vault, so a lost disk does not mean lost keys.

Backups

Every change is copied to the folders you choose, optionally to a server of your own, and — if you turn it on — to your own Google Drive.

Two-factor codes from a QR code

Reads the QR code from the screen, an image or the phone’s camera, including the export from Google Authenticator.

Private by design

The vault is encrypted with AES-256 on your device. Nothing is sent to the author — there is no Keyhold server, no account and no tracking. Read how Keyhold protects your data and the privacy policy.
